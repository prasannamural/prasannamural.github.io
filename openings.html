How to Apply

Writing a strong email.

I read every email. A strong introduction has three things: (a) a specific paper or project of ours that caught your attention and why, (b) a short note on your own experience — techniques you've used, a result you're proud of, (c) a CV attached as PDF.

I try to reply within two weeks. If you haven't heard from me, a polite nudge is welcome — but please don't send the same generic template I've seen a hundred times. Specificity is rare and enormously appreciated.

What to expect

Life in the lab.

We meet as a full group once a week, in smaller project clusters more often, and one-on-one as needed. Students are expected to present at a major conference each year and to publish in journals that match the rigor of the work. We value careful experimental practice, rigorous characterization, and writing that respects the reader.

Beyond the technical work, we try to keep the lab a warm place — we cook together, celebrate defenses loudly, and leave work at work on most weekends. If this sounds like the environment you're looking for, I'd love to hear from you.
